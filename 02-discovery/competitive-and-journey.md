# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** Persona: Mara the Hesitant Planner

Role: A shift worker with multiple open claims who wants to resolve debt digitally but has limited time and variable income.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Set up a repayment plan she can afford without making her financial situation worse.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** When asked to provide income information, Mara doesn't understand what information is required or why it is being collected, causing her to abandon the repayment-plan journey
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Google Search

"What income should I enter?"
"Why does a debt collector need my income?"
"What happens if I miss a payment?"

Banking App

Reviews recent deposits and salary payments.
Tries to estimate her "normal" income despite variable shift-work earnings.

Calculator / Spreadsheet (Excel, Google Sheets)

Manually calculates monthly income and expenses.
Tests whether a proposed payment might be affordable.

Payslips / Personal Documents

Searches for old salary statements to understand what figures to enter.

Friends and Family

Seeks reassurance before entering financial information or accepting a plan.

Phone Support / Collection Agent

Calls Riverty to ask questions that should be answered in the product itself.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Exit the repayment flow

Closes the portal and decides to come back later.

Gather income information

Opens banking apps and reviews payslips to estimate her "normal" monthly income.

Calculate affordability

Uses a calculator, notes app, or spreadsheet to compare income, expenses, and potential monthly payments.

Look for answers elsewhere

Searches Google or asks friends/family what income to enter and whether sharing the information is safe.

Contact customer support

Calls an agent to understand why income is needed and whether entering the wrong information could affect the outcome.
