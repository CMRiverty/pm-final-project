# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · BU Collection

**Scenario:** Riverty BU Collection, A Fair Way Forward

**Team:** [your assumed team size]

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** Mara the Hesitant Planner – a consumer with multiple open claims and variable income who wants to resolve debt digitally but lacks confidence in the repayment plan options.
- **Primary metric:** Increase repayment-plan agreement rate from 46% to 50%.
- **Moment of misery:** Consumers hesitate or abandon the journey because they do not understand why income information is requested and are unsure whether the repayment plan is affordable.
- **Guardrail:** Missed-payment rate within 60 days must not exceed the 19% baseline.

## Scoring
| Feature | Value | Effort | Quadrant | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 One plan for several open claims | 5 | 5 | Major Project | Next | Users with 2+ claims have the lowest agreement rates and highest agent-contact rates. |
| B2 Plain explainer for why income is asked | 5 | 1 | Quick Win | Now | Directly addresses a major source of confusion and agent contacts with minimal effort. |
| B3 Affordability check before plan options | 4 | 3 | Major Project | Next | Helps consumers choose sustainable plans and supports the repayment-completion goal. |
| B4 Handoff summary for the agent | 3 | 2 | Fill-In | Later | Improves support efficiency but does not directly address the primary user friction. |
| B5 Mobile photo upload fix / document skip | 5 | 4 | Major Project | Next | Known bug affecting many users at the largest funnel drop area. |
| B6 Pick payment date matching payday | 4 | 3 | Major Project | Next | Especially valuable for users with variable income and may reduce missed payments |
| B7 Show shortest plan first | 2 | 1 | Fill-In | Later | Increased agreements but also increased missed-payment rates, hurting the guardrail metric. |
| B8 Chat assistant on every page | 3 | 4 | Time Sinker | Cut | Broad improvement but weaker evidence than other opportunities. |
