# Plain explainer for why income is asked: Riverty BU Collection

**Case:** Riverty BU Collection, A Fair Way Forward
**Repo:** https://github.com/CMRiverty/pm-final-project

## Pitch

Consumers who need help the most are abandoning digital repayment plans because they don't understand or trust them, driving costly agent contacts, failed repayment outcomes and lost recovery value.

We scoped one feature, **B2 – Plain explainer for why income is asked**: a short reassurance text on the income-entry screen, with a full explanation one tap away (why income is requested, how it affects plan recommendations, how data is stored and protected, and why accurate income helps the customer).

- **Persona:** Mara the Hesitant Planner, a shift worker with multiple open claims and variable income who wants to set up a repayment plan digitally.
- **Primary metric:** repayment-plan agreement rate, 46% → 50% (+4 pp).
- **Guardrail:** 60-day missed-payment rate, baseline 19%, investigate at 20%.
- **Launch:** S tier (no outreach), conversion goal, owned channels only.

Note: the data packs are practice data, not Riverty research. The GTM plan assumes the A/B test meets its shipping criteria.
